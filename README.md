# 🏝️ TourLanka SL — Authentic Eco-Tourism & Experience Hub
> **SLIIT SE3090 — Software Engineering Frameworks (Mini Hackathon 2026)**  
> *Build for Sri Lanka — 4-Hour Time-boxed Sprint*

---

## 📌 1. Selected Sri Lankan Problem
Local Sri Lankan tour guides, village homestay hosts, and traditional tuk-tuk operators struggle to reach foreign and local tourists due to heavy **15-25% commission fees** charged by foreign booking aggregators. Meanwhile, tourists struggle to find verified, authentic local Sri Lankan eco-experiences.

---

## 💡 2. Proposed Digital Solution
**TourLanka SL** is a commission-free direct booking and discovery portal connecting tourists directly with verified Sri Lankan local guides, village homestays, and tuk-tuk drivers with location-based filtering (Ella, Sigiriya, Mirissa, Kandy) and authentic experience tags.
